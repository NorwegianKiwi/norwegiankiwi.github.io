Local representative flags
==========================

The following established local or unofficial representative flags are used
instead of the French tricolour so that each learnable place has a distinct
visual identity. Source files are from Wikimedia Commons.

French Guiana — "Flag of French Guiana.svg"
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_French_Guiana.svg
Public domain (released by the author).

Guadeloupe — "Unofficial flag of Guadeloupe (local).svg"
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Unofficial_flag_of_Guadeloupe_(local).svg
Public domain (ineligible simple design).

Réunion — "Proposed flag of Réunion (VAR).svg" (Lö Mahavéli)
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Proposed_flag_of_R%C3%A9union_(VAR).svg
Public domain (released by the author).

Mayotte — "Flag of Mayotte (local).svg"
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Flag_of_Mayotte_(local).svg
Public domain (Xrmap flag collection).
